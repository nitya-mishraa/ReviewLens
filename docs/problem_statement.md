# ReviewLens — Problem Statement

## Background

Online fashion shopping gives customers access to a large amount of
product information, including images, descriptions, ratings, reviews
and customer experiences.

However, having more information does not necessarily mean that
customers can make better decisions quickly.

---

## Problem

When evaluating a fashion product, shoppers often want to understand
what the product is actually like in real-world use — particularly its
fit, fabric quality, appearance and whether it matches their
expectations.

Although this information may already exist within customer reviews,
it is distributed across individual review comments and experiences.

Users therefore have to manually scan multiple reviews to identify
recurring patterns.

---

## User Pain Point

> "I want to know what customers actually experienced with this product,
> without having to read dozens or hundreds of reviews."

The problem is therefore not the absence of customer feedback.

It is the difficulty of converting a large volume of unstructured
feedback into a quick and useful understanding of the product.

---

## Problem Hypothesis

If customer reviews can be analysed and recurring product experiences
can be surfaced in a concise and trustworthy format, shoppers may be
able to evaluate products with less effort and greater confidence.

---

## Why This Matters

A better understanding of real customer experiences could potentially:

- Reduce uncertainty before purchase
- Reduce the effort required to evaluate reviews
- Help customers identify recurring product issues
- Improve the quality of purchase decisions
- Create actionable feedback signals for Myntra's product ecosystem

---

## Product Opportunity

ReviewLens explores whether customer review data can be transformed
from individual pieces of feedback into a structured
"Product Reality Snapshot."

Instead of asking the customer to interpret hundreds of reviews,
ReviewLens aims to surface the most important recurring experiences
in a concise format.

---

## Core Question

> Can Myntra turn the large amount of customer feedback it already
> collects into a faster, clearer and more actionable understanding
> of the real-world product experience?
