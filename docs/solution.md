# ReviewLens — Product Solution

## Solution Overview

ReviewLens is an interpretation layer built on top of existing customer
reviews.

Instead of requiring shoppers to manually read and interpret a large
number of reviews, ReviewLens identifies recurring customer experiences
and presents them as a concise Product Reality Snapshot.

---

## Core Experience

### Current Experience

Product Page
     ↓
Ratings + Reviews
     ↓
Customer manually scans reviews
     ↓
Tries to identify patterns
     ↓
Makes purchase decision


### ReviewLens Experience

Product Page
     ↓
Reviews are analysed
     ↓
Recurring experiences are identified
     ↓
Product Reality Snapshot
     ↓
Customer understands key patterns
     ↓
Makes purchase decision

---

## Product Reality Snapshot

The snapshot highlights recurring themes mentioned by customers.

Example:

### Product Reality Snapshot

⭐ Overall Rating: 4.2

**What customers experienced**

✓ Matches product appearance  
⚠ Fabric may feel thinner than expected  
✓ Comfortable for most users  
⚠ Some customers reported fit issues  

### Most Mentioned Concern

**Fabric thickness**

"Several recent customers mentioned that the fabric felt thinner
than expected."

[See supporting reviews]

---

## Key Design Principle

ReviewLens should summarize customer feedback, not hide it.

Every summarized insight should allow the user to access the underlying
reviews that support it.

This keeps the experience transparent and allows customers to verify
the information themselves.

---

## What ReviewLens Does

1. Collects available customer review text.
2. Identifies recurring themes.
3. Groups similar experiences.
4. Determines the frequency of each theme.
5. Surfaces the most relevant product-level insights.
6. Links insights back to supporting reviews.

---

## What ReviewLens Does NOT Do

- Does not replace customer reviews.
- Does not generate fake customer opinions.
- Does not give a product a new rating.
- Does not claim that every customer had the same experience.
- Does not make the purchase decision for the customer.

---

## Product Goal

Reduce the cognitive effort required to understand a large set of
customer reviews while preserving access to the original customer
feedback.
