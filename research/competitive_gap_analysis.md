# ReviewLens — Competitive & Gap Analysis

## Existing Myntra Experience

Myntra already provides several sources of product information:

- Product ratings
- Customer reviews
- Customer-uploaded photos
- Product descriptions
- Size and fit information
- Product attributes

These features give shoppers substantial information before making a
purchase.

---

## The Identified Gap

The problem is not the absence of information.

The problem is the effort required to interpret that information.

A product may have hundreds of reviews, but important experiences can
remain distributed across individual comments.

For example:

Review 1 → "Fabric is very thin"

Review 2 → "Looks good but material is thinner than expected"

Review 3 → "Expected a thicker fabric"

Review 4 → "Colour is slightly different"

The shopper has to manually connect these reviews to understand whether
a recurring pattern exists.

---

## Current Experience vs ReviewLens

| Current Myntra Experience | ReviewLens |
|---|---|
| Individual reviews | Recurring patterns across reviews |
| Overall rating | Aspect-level signals |
| Customer manually scans feedback | Key insights surfaced upfront |
| Information is distributed | Information is structured |
| User interprets the reviews | Product helps surface patterns |
| Reviews remain the primary source | Reviews remain the source + insights act as an interpretation layer |

---

## Why ReviewLens Is Not Another Review Feature

ReviewLens does not replace:

- Ratings
- Reviews
- Customer photos
- Product descriptions
- Existing fit information

Instead, it adds an interpretation layer on top of existing customer
feedback.

The goal is to answer:

> "What are customers consistently saying about this product?"

without requiring the shopper to manually process a large volume of
reviews.

## Strategic Opportunity

If validated successfully, ReviewLens could create value at two levels:

### Customer

- Reduce effort spent reading reviews
- Surface important product experiences faster
- Improve purchase confidence
- Make product evaluation more transparent

### Myntra

- Better utilisation of existing customer feedback
- Earlier visibility into recurring product issues
- Potentially improve product experience and customer trust
- Create structured feedback signals that could inform future product
  and marketplace decisions

---

## Core Differentiation

> ReviewLens is not another source of reviews.

> **It is a layer that converts existing customer feedback into
> understandable product intelligence.**
