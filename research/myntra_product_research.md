# Myntra Product Research

## 1. Existing Product Experience

Myntra already provides customers with:
- Product ratings and reviews
- Customer-uploaded photos
- Product descriptions
- Fit and size-related information
- Product details such as fabric and material

## 2. Existing Customer Feedback

Customers can leave reviews describing their actual experience with:
- Product quality
- Fit and size
- Colour/appearance
- Fabric
- Product-photo accuracy
- Delivery and other post-order issues

## 3. Current Gap

Although this information exists, useful customer insights are distributed across individual reviews.

A shopper may need to manually read multiple reviews to understand recurring product-level issues.

## 4. Product Opportunity

Can Myntra convert scattered customer feedback into a concise, trustworthy summary that helps shoppers understand the "reality" of a product before purchasing?

## 5. Supporting Data

### Fit & Return Problem

Myntra has historically identified sizing and fit as a significant
source of fashion returns.

The Economic Times reported that around half of fashion/apparel returns
were attributed to unsatisfactory size, highlighting the difficulty of
getting fit right in online fashion shopping.

This provides external evidence that product expectations and
fit-related uncertainty can have a meaningful impact on the fashion
e-commerce experience.

**Source:** The Economic Times / publicly reported Myntra sizing and
returns initiatives.

### Myntra Consumer Grievances

The National Consumer Helpline's public company-wise grievance
dashboard also shows recurring consumer complaints associated with
Myntra.

For January-August 2025, the dashboard reported:

| Grievance Category | Complaints |
|---|---:|
| Delivery of Wrong Product | 3,431 |
| Refund/Replacement not provided | 2,116 |
| Non-Delivery of Product | 1,434 |
| Delivery of Defective/Damaged Product | 1,128 |
| Wrong information/specification on website | 136 |

**Source:** National Consumer Helpline, Myntra Designs Private Limited
company-wise grievance dashboard.

---

### What This Data Tells Us

These sources do not directly measure the potential impact of
ReviewLens.

Instead, they establish that customer experience problems around
product fit, product accuracy, delivery and post-purchase issues exist
at meaningful scale.

Combined with our informal user interviews, this strengthens the
problem discovery:

- Users actively use reviews to reduce purchase uncertainty.
- Users care about real-world product attributes such as fit and
  fabric.
- Users generally do not read every review.
- Public data shows that product and post-purchase issues are not
  isolated incidents.

This creates an opportunity to investigate whether the information
already contained in customer reviews can be converted into a faster,
more useful product-level signal.

---

### Research Limitation

The grievance figures above are secondary research sourced from a
public government dashboard. They were not collected directly by me.

Similarly, the return/fit statistic is based on publicly reported
industry/company information.

These figures are therefore used to establish problem scale and
context, not to claim that ReviewLens will directly reduce returns.

ReviewLens' impact would need to be validated through controlled
experimentation after implementation.
### Research Methodology Note

The figures above are secondary research sourced from publicly
available reporting and the National Consumer Helpline's company-wise
grievance dashboard. They were not independently collected or
calculated as part of this case study.

They are used to provide external context and validate the scale of
customer-experience problems. They are not presented as evidence of
ReviewLens' potential impact, which would require product-level
experimentation.
