# Myntra Product Research

## 1. Existing Product Experience

Myntra already provides customers with:
- Product ratings and reviews
- Customer-uploaded photos
- Product descriptions
- Fit and size-related information
- Product details such as fabric and material

## 2. Existing Customer Feedback

Customers can leave reviews describing their actual experience with:
- Product quality
- Fit and size
- Colour/appearance
- Fabric
- Product-photo accuracy
- Delivery and other post-order issues

## 3. Current Gap

Although this information exists, useful customer insights are distributed across individual reviews.

A shopper may need to manually read multiple reviews to understand recurring product-level issues.

## 4. Product Opportunity

Can Myntra convert scattered customer feedback into a concise, trustworthy summary that helps shoppers understand the "reality" of a product before purchasing?
