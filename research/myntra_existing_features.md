# Myntra Existing Features vs ReviewLens

## What Myntra Already Provides

Myntra already gives shoppers several sources of product information:

| Existing Feature | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Product Ratings | Overall customer rating |
| Customer Reviews | Individual customer experiences |
| Customer Photos | Real-world visual references |
| Product Description | Seller/brand-provided product information |
| Fit / Size Information | Information related to sizing and fit |
| Product Attributes | Details such as material, colour, etc. |

---

## The Gap

The problem is not that Myntra lacks customer feedback.

The problem is that the feedback is primarily presented as individual
pieces of information.

For example, a shopper may find:

Review 1 → "Fabric is thin"

Review 2 → "Looks good but material is thinner than expected"

Review 3 → "Expected thicker fabric"

Review 4 → "Colour is slightly different"

The customer has to manually connect these individual reviews and
identify whether a recurring pattern exists.

---

## ReviewLens

ReviewLens does not replace Myntra's existing reviews.

Instead, it acts as an interpretation layer on top of existing
customer feedback.

### Current Experience

Hundreds of reviews
        ↓
Customer manually scans reviews
        ↓
Customer identifies patterns
        ↓
Customer makes purchase decision

### ReviewLens Experience

Customer reviews
        ↓
ReviewLens analyses recurring themes
        ↓
Key product experiences are surfaced
        ↓
Customer makes a more informed decision

---

## Product Differentiation

| Myntra Today | ReviewLens |
|---|---|
| Shows individual reviews | Surfaces recurring patterns |
| User manually scans feedback | Reduces information-processing effort |
| Rating gives an overall signal | Provides aspect-level signals |
| Information is distributed | Information is structured |
| Customer interprets the feedback | Product helps interpret the feedback |

---

## Important Principle

ReviewLens is not intended to replace reviews.

It is intended to make the information already present in reviews
easier to discover and understand.

The product question is:

> "How can we help a shopper understand 100 reviews without requiring
> them to read all 100 reviews?"
